Privacy Policy — Wolvex TikTok App

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

1. What data this app touches

The "Wolvex" app is a posting tool for one TikTok channel (@wolvex20), owned by the same person who runs it. It touches only:

  • The TikTok account authorization granted by the owner through TikTok's own OAuth sign-in (needed to publish clips).
  • The video clips the owner chooses to post, and the captions the owner writes for them.

2. What we do not collect

We collect no personal data from anyone else — no names, emails, locations, or browsing data of any third party. We store no passwords. We run no analytics, advertising, or tracking on this website.

3. Sharing

Nothing is shared with third parties except what is necessary to publish: the clip and its caption are sent to TikTok's Content Posting API so the video appears on the owner's TikTok channel. TikTok handles that data under its own privacy policy.

4. Data retention and removal

The app keeps no copy of anyone's personal data. The owner can revoke the app's TikTok authorization at any time from their TikTok account settings, which immediately stops the app from posting.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: openworldwolf@gmail.com.
