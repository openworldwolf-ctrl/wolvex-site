Terms of Service — Wolvex TikTok App

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

1. What this app does

The "Wolvex" app posts the owner's own original GTA 6 clips to the owner's own TikTok channel (@wolvex20) using TikTok's Content Posting API. The clips are the owner's own edits.

2. Who can use it

This app is for the Wolvex channel owner only. It is not offered to the public and it does not post to anyone else's account. Only the owner authorizes it, through TikTok's own OAuth sign-in.

3. Content

All clips posted through the app are the owner's own original edits of GTA 6 gameplay and streamer moments. The app does not copy, re-upload or claim anyone else's work, and it does not post content the owner did not create.

4. Data

The app collects no personal data from anyone else. It touches only the TikTok account authorization and the clips the owner chooses to post. See our Privacy Policy.

5. Availability

The app is provided as-is. Posting depends on TikTok's API and on the owner's TikTok account staying in good standing. TikTok's own terms apply to everything posted.
